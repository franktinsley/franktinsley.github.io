How I work

This page is
the demo.

Everything behind this text is one signed-distance field, solved per pixel at full device resolution, every frame. Most of the time, making something more beautiful and making it easier to use are the same change — that's the one I aim for. My judgment about that is visual: get a rough version on screen early, look at it hard, change it, look again — then harden it until it holds frame rate on the machine it actually ships on.

Technical Artist on Meta's avatar system for VR and mixed reality, on avatar rendering and real-time interactivity. Designed and built a visual in-editor tool for authoring mock events and driving them at runtime, so the live-avatar systems could be tested behaviorally instead of by hand.

This page

The background you're reading over: one signed-distance field, no libraries, lit by a single baked studio environment, tracking your cursor or your finger at full device resolution. Two WebGL2 programs draw it — one scrolls, one is pinned — sharing the field function verbatim so the seam doesn't exist. Live frame counter in the readout.
